    <h3 class="cfp-h3">2026 Commentators Needed</h3>
    <p class="text-justify">Commentators are an essential part of philosophy conferences! As a commentator, you will provide two very important things:</p>
    <ol>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5em;">Summary & Analysis: You'll summarize and analyze your fellow philosopher's argument to help the audience better understand it.</li>
      <li style="margin-bottom: 0.5em;">Philosophical Critique: You'll offer your fellow philosopher critical questions aimed at helping them improve and expand their argument.</li>
    </ol>
    <p>Check out <a href="{{ site.baseurl }}/advice/commentators">Dr. Koolage's Advice for Commentators</a> for more details about what commentators do, and how to do it.</p>
    <p>If you are a <strong>current undergraduate student</strong> and want to volunteer, email <a href="mailto:{{ 'emuucip@gmail.com' | encode_email }}?subject=2026%20UCiP%20Commentator%20Volunteering" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" role="button">emuucip@email.com</a>. Include your <strong>full name</strong> and <strong>current university affiliation</strong> or you will not be considered.</p>
    <div class="text-center">
      <a class="btn btn-primary cfp" href="mailto:{{ 'emuucip@gmail.com' | encode_email }}?subject=2026%20UCiP%20Commentator%20Volunteering" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer" role="button">Email Us to Volunteer</a>
    </div>
    <h4>Eligibility</h4>
    <p>This is an undergraduate conference. Only current undergraduate students are eligible to provide commentary at the UCiP. Professional philosophers, graduate students, or others holding an advanced degree (in philosophy) will not be considered as commentators.</p>
